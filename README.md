# Melaka 2026 — Fourteen English Candidate Introduction Packs

Each of the **14 independent candidate folders** contains a self-contained `SKILL.md` and `PROMPT.md` with all-English character-generation, voiceover, six four-panel storyboard image, 24 individual video and six continuous video prompts.

**Total:** 14 candidates · 336 × 2.5-second portrait scenes · 84 single-image storyboard prompts · 336 independent video prompts · 84 optional continuous video scripts.

## Character reference-free testing

No photo or image upload is required. Each candidate has a detailed one-shot written identity specification. To assess reproducibility, independently generate Design A and Design B from the *same text prompt* without referencing either output in the second generation. Record a feature-by-feature comparison; text-only character generation **cannot guarantee the same face**. Optional use of the first generated design as a visual reference is permitted only for production, not as proof of no-reference consistency.

**Important:** Images are AI editorial interpretations, not authentic or verified photographic likenesses. Neutral election information, no voter persuasion or invented speeches. Add an on-screen disclosure: **AI-generated character and voice; not genuine candidate footage or statements.** 

The election facts are a 2 November 2026 research snapshot and must be verified before publication. Claims from parties or individual candidates are attributed, plans are not described as delivered, and broader programme results are not credited to one individual alone.
